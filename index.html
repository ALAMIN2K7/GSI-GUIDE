GGSI-GUIDE

A simple and reliable guide for installing Android GSIs.

COMPLETE INSTALLATION GUIDE

Prepare. Check. Flash.

Follow each step carefully before flashing a GSI. Make sure your device supports Project Treble and keep a complete backup of your important data.

01
PHONE PREPARATION

Prepare your Android phone

Before connecting your phone to the PC, make sure the device is properly prepared for GSI installation.

01
Unlock the Bootloader

Your device bootloader must be unlocked before flashing a custom system image.

02
Enable Developer Options

Open Settings → About Phone and tap Build Number several times to enable Developer Options.

03
Enable USB Debugging

Go to Developer Options and enable USB Debugging.

04
Check Project Treble Support

Verify that your phone supports Project Treble before downloading a GSI.

05
Back Up Your Data

GSI installation may erase userdata. Back up photos, files, contacts and other important data.

03
FLASHING PROCESS

How to install the GSI

Once your phone and PC are ready, follow the flashing sequence carefully. Do not disconnect the USB cable while commands are being executed.

01
Connect the phone

Connect the unlocked Android phone to your PC using a reliable USB cable.

02
Open Platform Tools

Open a Terminal or Command Prompt inside the folder containing ADB and Fastboot.

03
Enter FastbootD

Reboot the phone into FastbootD mode and verify that Fastboot detects the device.

04
Prepare the partitions

Follow the command sequence below to prepare the dynamic partitions when required.

05
Flash the GSI

Flash the compatible GSI image to the system partition using Fastboot.

06
Wipe userdata

Erase userdata when required by the GSI installation process.

07
Reboot

Reboot the phone and wait patiently for the first boot.

FLASHING

Install the GSI

Run the following Fastboot commands in order to flash the GSI. Make sure you understand each command before executing it.

Step 01
Reboot the connected phone from Android into FastbootD mode
adb reboot fastboot
Step 02
Re-enter FastbootD mode using the Fastboot interface
fastboot reboot fastboot
Step 03
Flash the stock vbmeta image with verification disabled
fastboot --disable-verity --disable-verification flash vbmeta vbmeta.img
Step 04
Erase the existing system partition before flashing the GSI
fastboot erase system
Step 05
Delete the product logical partition to free dynamic partition space
fastboot delete-logical-partition product_a
Step 06
Delete the alternate product logical partition
fastboot delete-logical-partition product_b
Step 07
Flash the GSI image to the system partition
fastboot flash system gsi.img
Step 08
Erase user data before the first boot
fastboot erase userdata
Step 09
Reboot the phone into Android after flashing
fastboot reboot
!

Before You Flash

Make sure your bootloader is unlocked, your device supports Project Treble, and the GSI matches your device architecture and Android version. Keep a complete backup of your important data before starting the installation.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Not enough space?

Some dynamic-partition layouts may require additional space before flashing a GSI.

!Not enough space to resize partition

If Fastboot reports FAILED (remote: 'Not enough space to resize partition'), the following logical partitions may need to be removed before flashing the GSI, depending on the device layout.

fastboot delete-logical-partition system_ext
fastboot delete-logical-partition system_ext_a
fastboot delete-logical-partition system_ext_b
fastboot delete-logical-partition product
fastboot delete-logical-partition product_a
fastboot delete-logical-partition product_b
